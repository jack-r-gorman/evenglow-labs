Sunset LM privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Sunset LM is made by Evenglow Labs. This is the same text as Settings > Privacy policy in the app.

Who we are

Sunset LM is made by Evenglow Labs.

The short version

Sunset runs its AI model on your phone. Your messages, photos and files are processed on the device and are not sent to us. We do not run servers that receive your conversations, and the app contains no advertising or analytics. Voice input is the one exception to read about below.

What stays on your phone

Conversations, memories the assistant saves about you, attachments, settings and downloaded models are stored in the app's storage on this device. Deleting a chat, clearing all chats or uninstalling the app removes them, except a model you chose to save to your Files folder.

Microphone and camera

The microphone is used only while you are dictating or on a voice call. Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (on most Android phones, Google's). Depending on your phone, that service may process audio on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy. Typing never involves it. The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo to attach.

When the app uses the internet

Only to download a model you choose (from Hugging Face), and for two optional features that are off unless you turn them on in Settings: connecting to your own Ollama server, and syncing chats to your own Supabase project. In those cases data goes only to the server you configured.

Reporting a response

If you report an AI response, the report is saved on your phone. If email reporting is enabled, your mail app opens with the response text so you can choose to send it to us.

Children

Sunset is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with an app update. Questions: evenglowlabs@gmail.com.
